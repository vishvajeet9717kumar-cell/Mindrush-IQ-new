Privacy Policy for MindRush IQ

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Welcome to MindRush IQ ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you play MindRush IQ on web browsers and mobile devices.

1. Information We Collect

MindRush IQ is an offline-capable educational brain training arcade game. We do not require account registration, usernames, or passwords:

  • Local Game Data: Your high scores, accumulated coins, unlocked themes, missions, and sound preferences are stored strictly on your local device using standard HTML5 LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you clear your browser cache.
  • Device & Usage Information: When accessing the game via the web or Google Play, standard non-identifying telemetry (such as browser type, operating system, and crash diagnostics) may be processed to ensure gameplay performance.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense & AdMob)

We may display advertisements from third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense and Google AdMob, to support free access to the game:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MindRush IQ is designed for general audiences and offers educational math, vocabulary, and pattern training puzzles. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Security

Because your gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can erase all game progress at any time by clearing your browser's site data or uninstalling the app.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
